Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

I-Planner is an AI-powered productivity and personal planning application that helps users manage tasks, goals, study plans, career planning, financial goals, and related activities.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, profile details, tasks and goals, uploaded documents, AI interactions, calendar connections, notification preferences, and device information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address) from that provider, and use them to create and identify your account.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve the app, generate AI-powered plans and recommendations, sync calendars, send reminders, provide customer support, and maintain security.

AI and User Data

AI Coach, syllabus parsing, and goal/exam planning features are powered by OpenAI's API. When you use these features, we send OpenAI: your message text, and — only for the categories you've allowed in Settings → AI Data Access — your task titles and due dates, goal titles and milestones, and calendar/class/exam titles and times. Before AI Coach ever sends your first message, the app shows you exactly what would be shared and asks you to agree. You may revoke access to any data category, or any AI feature, in Settings at any time; nothing already sent to OpenAI can be recalled, but no further data is sent once revoked. OpenAI processes this data to generate a response and, per OpenAI's API data usage policy, does not use data submitted through its API to train its models. OpenAI is contractually required to protect this data to a standard consistent with this policy.

Google User Data (Google Calendar)

If you choose to connect your Google Calendar, I-Planner asks Google for permission to view and manage events on your calendars (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope). You can use I-Planner without connecting Google Calendar, and you decide whether to connect it.

What we access. Your calendar events: their titles, dates, times, and the identifiers needed to keep them in sync. We do not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Photos, or any other Google data.

How we use it. Only to provide the calendar features you see in the app: showing your events alongside your tasks, importing events as tasks, and adding or updating events in your calendar for tasks you choose to sync. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Limited Use. I-Planner's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, the use of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • We do not use Google user data, raw or derived, to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not allow others to do so.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except as needed to provide the features you use (see "AI and User Data" below), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or sale of assets with notice to you.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data, unless you ask us to for support, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or it is required by law.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting or interest-based advertising.

AI features. When you use AI Coach or planning features, calendar event titles and times may be sent to OpenAI to generate your response, but only if you have allowed the calendar category in Settings → AI Data Access, and only to produce that response for you. You can turn this off at any time. OpenAI's API does not use data submitted through it to train its models, and we do not use your Google data to train any model.

Storage and protection. Your Google access token is stored encrypted on our servers and transmitted over HTTPS. Access is limited to what is needed to operate the service.

Disconnecting and deletion. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Profile → Calendar Sync; doing so stops syncing and revokes the access we were granted. You can also remove I-Planner's access from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting your I-Planner account removes your stored Google connection and the calendar data we hold for you.

Financial Information

Financial planning features are for educational and organizational purposes only and are not investment, tax, legal, or professional financial advice.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we never sell or use Google user data for advertising. We share data with service providers that help operate the app: OpenAI (AI features, described above), Firebase (authentication), hosting and database providers, and Apple/Google (in-app purchase processing). Each is bound to use your data only to provide their service to us, not for their own independent purposes.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage where appropriate.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Data received from Google is kept only while your Google Calendar stays connected and your account exists; it is removed when you disconnect or delete your account.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your account data, subject to applicable law.

Children

I-Planner is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information promptly.
