Delete Your I-Planner Account

I-Planner is developed by Obito Ventures Inc.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the I-Planner app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete account.
  4. Type the confirmation phrase shown on screen, then tap Delete account to finish.

This permanently and immediately deletes your account and data — there is no recovery period.

Option 2 — Request deletion without opening the app

If you can't or don't want to sign in, email obitoventuresinc@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account." We'll verify the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Both options permanently delete:

  • Your account itself (email, name, sign-in credentials)
  • All tasks, goals, habits, study/exam plans, and classes
  • AI Coach conversation history
  • Uploaded syllabus files and any data extracted from them
  • Subscription and purchase records, and AI usage history
  • Calendar sync and notification settings

What's retained

Nothing tied to your identity is kept after deletion. Standard server access logs (used only for security and abuse prevention) may persist briefly under normal hosting retention before automatic rotation, but are not linked back to your deleted account.
